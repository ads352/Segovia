Build the Aqueduct: Kahoot polls

Group E, EMBA Self-Leadership Week, Segovia. 3 poll questions for a live game.

Instructions for creating this Kahoot
  • Create exactly 3 questions. Do not add, remove or merge questions.
  • Question type: Poll. There is no correct answer and no points. If Poll is not available, use Quiz and mark all four answers as correct.
  • Use the question and answer text exactly as written. Do not rewrite or shorten it.
  • Keep the answers in the order shown: Answer 1 (red triangle), Answer 2 (blue diamond), Answer 3 (yellow circle), Answer 4 (green square). Do not shuffle answers.
  • Time limit: 20 seconds per question. No images needed.

All 3 questions are on page 2, which also repeats the key rules, so page 2 can be used on its own.

Create a 3-question Kahoot. Poll type, no correct answer (or Quiz with all four answers correct). Use the text exactly as written. Keep the answer order: 1 red, 2 blue, 3 yellow, 4 green. Do not shuffle. 20 seconds each.

Question 1

You asked Marta. She says: "I don't feel I can disagree with you in meetings. You've decided before we talk."

Answer 1From tomorrow, ask for everyone's view before you give yours.
Answer 2Change how you run meetings, and at the next one say why.
Answer 3Explain you always ask for input. Encourage her to speak up more.
Answer 4Thank her warmly. Tell her this is really useful to hear.

Question 2

You asked the team. Rui says: "Our daily stand-up runs 40 minutes. It eats my best focus time." What do you do?

Answer 1Agree it's long. Promise to look at it after the release.
Answer 2Show him the data: since daily stand-ups started, rework is down.
Answer 3Cut it to 15 min, credit Rui, and check in two weeks if it works.
Answer 4Cut it to 15 minutes, starting Monday.

Question 3

You asked Priya. She says: "I made a mistake on the drawings last week. I was afraid to tell you." What do you do?

Answer 1Thank her at the team meeting for raising it, share the new review step.
Answer 2Add a second review step to the process.
Answer 3Reassure her: it's fine, everyone makes mistakes.
Answer 4Find out who reviewed the drawings, so it doesn't happen again.
