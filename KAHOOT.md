# Kahoot set-up for Build the Aqueduct

Run Kahoot and the game side by side on the laptop driving the projector.
The game's `K` key (or opening `index.html#kahoot`) switches it to Kahoot mode.

## Kahoot settings
- Question type: **Poll** (no right answer, no points). If your plan has no Poll, use Quiz and mark all four answers correct.
- Keep the answer order exactly as below. **Do not shuffle answers.** Red = 1, Blue = 2, Yellow = 3, Green = 4 in the game.
- Time limit: 20 seconds per question.
- No intro or lobby music questions. Just the 3 polls.

## On the day
1. Before the talk: open Kahoot on the left half of the screen and the game on the right half.
2. Launch the Kahoot lobby while you do the intro. Ask people to join as you start talking.
3. Each round: Presenter A reads the feedback, the room votes in Kahoot.
4. When Kahoot shows the result, Presenter B **clicks** the winning colour in the game.
   If you clicked in Kahoot, `1`–`4` go to Kahoot, so click the game first before using keys.
5. Tie: Presenter B picks the higher number. It keeps the game moving.

## Questions (all under Kahoot's limits: 120 characters per question, 75 per answer)

### Round 1 — Marta, senior engineer
**Q:** Marta: "I don't feel I can disagree with you in meetings. You've decided before we talk." What do you do?

| Shape | Answer |
|---|---|
| 🔺 Red | Defend: "I always ask for input. People should speak up more." |
| 🔷 Blue | Thank her. Nothing changes. |
| 🟡 Yellow | Ask for views first in meetings. Don't mention it. |
| 🟩 Green | Change how you run meetings, then tell the team Marta raised it. |

### Round 2 — Rui, design engineer
**Q:** Rui: "Our daily stand-up runs 40 minutes. It eats my best focus time." What do you do?

| Shape | Answer |
|---|---|
| 🔺 Red | Defend with data: "Alignment prevents rework." |
| 🔷 Blue | "Fair. Let's look at it at some point." |
| 🟡 Yellow | Cut it to 15 minutes. No explanation. |
| 🟩 Green | Cut to 15 min. "Rui flagged this. Tell me in two weeks if it works." |

### Round 3 — Priya, graduate engineer
**Q:** Priya: "I made a mistake on the drawings last week. I was afraid to tell you." What do you do?

| Shape | Answer |
|---|---|
| 🔺 Red | "That should have been caught. Who checked it?" |
| 🔷 Blue | "OK, thanks for telling me." |
| 🟡 Yellow | Fix the review step. Keep it between you. |
| 🟩 Green | Thank her in front of the team, fix the review step, report back. |
